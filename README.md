# SHARE Life History Data Harmonization & Analysis

## Overview

This project summarizes data construction and analysis work completed as part of my role as a Graduate Research Assistant at the University of Michigan. The project uses longitudinal data from the Survey of Health, Ageing and Retirement in Europe (SHARE) and focuses on harmonizing SHARE Life History measures with comparable measures from the Health and Retirement Study (HRS).

The work involved transforming complex retrospective life-history data into analysis-ready measures across residential, education, health, partnership, and employment domains.

## Key Contributions

- Cleaned and harmonized longitudinal SHARE Life History data.
- Constructed analysis-ready variables from age-specific residential, employment, and partnership histories.
- Developed reproducible workflows for variable construction and data management.
- Investigated data-quality and record-linkage issues between SHARELIFE and core SHARE datasets.
- Applied validation rules and handled complex missing-data structures.
- Conducted weighted descriptive analyses across Germany, Italy, Sweden, and Switzerland.
- Created visualizations examining residential mobility across the life course.

## Data Quality & Validation

A key component of the project involved investigating unmatched records between SHARELIFE and the core SHARE data. Rather than treating unmatched observations as simple merge failures, respondent identifiers, survey participation patterns, household information, and partner identifiers were examined to identify potential explanations for the discrepancies.

This process helped distinguish likely differences in survey participation and sampling from data-processing errors.

## Example Analysis

One component of the analysis examined residential mobility from ages 0 through 50. Age-specific and life-stage measures were constructed from respondents' residential histories, and weighted estimates were used to compare mobility patterns across countries and education groups.

The analysis demonstrated how harmonized longitudinal data can be transformed into interpretable measures for cross-country research.

## Data Availability

The underlying SHARE data are not included in this repository. Access to SHARE data is subject to the terms and conditions established by the data provider.

This repository is intended to demonstrate the data-management, validation, and analytical methods used in the project without distributing restricted research data.
